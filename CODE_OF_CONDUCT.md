# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive project community.
Everyone participating in Structify AI is expected to communicate professionally
and focus on improving the software and documentation.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Welcome useful feedback and different viewpoints.
* Keep discussions focused on the project.
* Report issues clearly and in good faith.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
* Discriminatory language or behavior
* Publishing private information without permission
* Disruptive or bad-faith participation

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate discussions, close issues, remove comments, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.
